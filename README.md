# 🦄 HAPAning – AI Finance Dashboard

> **Making Finance Happen. Smart. Simple. AI Powered.**

HAPAning is a modern **AI-powered finance dashboard** designed for Gen Z investors. Built with a sleek **Glassmorphism UI**, it simulates market insights, portfolio allocation, and TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) execution strategies to help users understand investing concepts in an interactive way.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://hapaing-unicorn.netlify.app

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔥 Sector Heat Map
- Interactive visualization of market sectors.
- Color-coded performance indicators:
  - 🟢 Positive
  - 🔴 Negative
  - 🟡 Neutral
- Displays overall market mood.
- Highlights top-performing sectors.

---

### 🤖 AI Fund Allocator
Simulate AI-based portfolio allocation based on current market momentum.

**Features**
- Enter your investment amount.
- AI suggests sector-wise fund distribution.
- AI Confidence Score.
- Interactive Doughnut Chart visualization.
- Beginner-friendly investment insights.

---

### ⏳ TWAP Simulator
Learn how institutional investors execute large orders using the **Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP)** strategy.

**Features**
- Choose a market sector.
- Enter investment amount.
- Configure execution duration.
- Compare TWAP execution with lump-sum buying.
- Visualizes estimated entry price, volatility savings, and expected performance.

---

### 📊 Portfolio Dashboard
- Investment summary
- Equity overview
- Profit/Loss tracking
- Holdings table
- Market indices
- Investment products section
